# Analysis of the reference reel

Source: an Instagram reel by the reference creator, promising the 5 settings they would set up if they had to configure Claude in a single day. 720 × 1280, 30 fps, 75.5 s.
ElevenLabs Scribe v1 transcript: `transcription_elevenlabs.txt`.

## Measurements

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|
| Spoken duration | 74.1 s for 300 words, so 4 words/s (243 words/min), very fast |
| Hard cuts | None: a single continuous shot, everything transforms in place |
| Music | None measurable (-55 to -65 dB in the pauses): dry voice and small sound effects |
| Sound | Loudness range 2.9 LU, peak -1.5 dBTP |
| Visual pace | A change on screen every 1 to 2 s, each appearance lands on the spoken word |
| Face | None: a mascot plays the presenter |

## The formula of an element

1. **Name**: ordinal + short name.
2. **Definition** in one simple sentence.
3. **Concrete benefit** with an example or a list of 3 or 4 cases.
4. **Punchline**: a short sentence that closes the idea (two clicks and Claude gets good in that area).

## The visual grammar

- **Single canvas**: the background never changes, nothing is cut, things transform. The viewer never gets an "it's over" signal.
- **One bright color**: everything is black and white except the mascot. The eye knows where to go.
- **Mascot at the bottom**: always alive, it gives a presence without a face.
- **Segmented progress bar** at the top, one segment per element: you know how much is left, which keeps you watching.
- **Fixed header**: numbered badge, small monospace line, title with the key word highlighted.
- **Schematic app window**: the interface is suggested, the screen is not filmed.
- **Word-by-word captions**: two or three words, the current word on a pill.
- **Tilted stamps** on numbers and relaunches.

## The retention levers

- Open loop made visible by the 5 empty boxes: the brain wants to see them filled.
- Blame flipped: it is not the tool, it is you, and it can be fixed.
- Rising difficulty, rising value, the best at the end.
- "But wait" right before the last element, when attention drops.
- Every sentence triggers an appearance: never a still screen for more than 2 s.
- Call to action shown as a series of gestures, not just said.

## What we keep and what we change for our reel

| We keep | We change |
|---|---|
| Promise, open loop, 5 elements, relaunch, recap, call to action | The hook: lived proof ("this voice is not me") instead of a promise |
| Continuous canvas, progress bar, header, window, word-by-word captions | The look: the creator's navy and mint palette, word-by-word captions with a highlighted pill |
| Presenter mascot | Our own mascot, whose mouth follows the voice, and which multiplies in element 5 |
| Schematic interfaces | Real traces of how this video was made (files, commands, connectors, skill) |
| Tilted stamps | A cinematic opening shot generated with Atlas Cloud, and a reel that loops on itself |
