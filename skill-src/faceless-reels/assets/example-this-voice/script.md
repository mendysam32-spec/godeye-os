## Explanation

**It acts.** First setting: the app on your computer. On the website, Claude answers you. On your computer, it acts. It opens your folders, it creates your files, it runs your programs. It built this video directly on my Mac.

**It connects.** Second setting: connectors. In a few clicks, Claude connects to your tools. Your email, your calendar, Notion. I connected it to Eleven Labs. It wrote its text, sent it, and got back this voice. All by itself.

**It learns.** Third setting: skills. A skill is your method, written down once and for all. Claude learns it, and applies it every single time. I put my editing style in it: my colors, my captions, my sound.

**It remembers.** Fourth setting: instructions. You tell it once how you work. Your language, your style, what you never want to see again. It remembers, in every conversation.

**It multiplies.** And the fifth one is where it gets crazy: parallel agents. Claude multiplies. One agent writes a scene, another builds the next one, a third checks everything. At the same time.
